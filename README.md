# coolest-art-01 Entry: Robot Mending a Star on the Moon

## The Artwork
A cute small friendly robot with round glowing eyes sits on a luminous
crescent moon, carefully stitching a torn glowing star back together with
golden thread. Deep blue night sky, twinkling stars, soft pastel clouds.
Luminous storybook style, charming and quirky — sticker art a developer
would love on their laptop.

## How it runs
`python3 main.py` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png` (deterministic,
stdlib only, no network). Two runs produce identical bytes.
